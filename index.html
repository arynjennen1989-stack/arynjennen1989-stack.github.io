Find a name that costs $0. Not a leftover .com.

There is no public dump of abandoned .com names you can grab for free. Freenom’s free .tk factory shut down in 2024. What still exists: programs that hand out a subdomain, or a third-level name, at no charge.

This site lists those programs and checks a few of them live. It does not mass-register names, does not scrape registrar aftermarkets, and does not sell “expired premium” junk.
