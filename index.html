Lost, dead, or cheap. Then actually grab it.

Type a word. We ask the official registry (RDAP) if that name is still unregistered on cheap endings, check still-free programs, and hand you the claim link. $0 forever still only exists as subdomains and eu.org. A real TLD is year-1 cheap, not free.

Expired .coms go to auction. We do not scrape those lists. “Open” on .xyz/.icu/.top means you can register it — often about a dollar the first year, then a renewal. Re-check the price on TLD-List before you pay. We never register names for you. Registrar buttons are search links, not an affiliate cookie, until an account is added.
