About

Week One Gigs is a small site for people who need a first remote paycheck. It is run by Aryn Jennen. There is no course, no coaching upsell, and no paid-follower scheme.

How we pick platforms

  • Free to join. If they charge testers or tutors a “training fee,” they are out.
  • Pay real money (PayPal or bank), not points you cannot cash.
  • Pay rates come from the platform’s own site when they publish one. Tester-reported ranges are labeled unverified.
  • We will add affiliate links later only for platforms we already recommend. Those will be marked.
