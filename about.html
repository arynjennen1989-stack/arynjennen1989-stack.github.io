About

Zero Dollar Domains is a small catalog run by Aryn Jennen. It lives on a free GitHub Pages host because that is also a $0 name.

What we gather

Two piles only: (1) programs that still give a name at no charge (eu.org, is-a.dev, github.io), and (2) names that official RDAP says are still unregistered on cheap TLDs. “Dead” on a taken name means no nameservers — not that you can take it.

What we do not gather

  • Expired .com / .net dumps and auction feeds. Those are not $0.
  • Freenom leftovers. Free registration ended in 2024.
  • Year-1 “free domain with hosting” deals presented as forever-free.
  • Anyone’s WHOIS or account data. We do not register names for you.

Start with the hunter, the startup graveyard, or the guides — especially why leftover .coms are not free and how to request eu.org.
